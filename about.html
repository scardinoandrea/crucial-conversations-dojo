About & privacy

About

A practice tool for the skills in Crucial Conversations (3rd edition). Two people get the same situation but different private facts, then talk it through using only the tools from the chapters they've actually read.

Practice with a colleague, or solo with Claude playing the other seat.

Built entirely with Claude, open source under the MIT license.

Privacy

  • No accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking.
  • Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. Debrief notes vanish when you close the tab.
  • Invite links carry only the case number, the file number and your chapter level, never your role.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Both see your IP address, as any website host does.
  • Solo mode runs inside your own Claude account. Those conversations follow Anthropic's terms and your account settings; this project never sees them.
